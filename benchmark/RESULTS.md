**Scam recall on the 20 challenge scams** (the metric that matters most for users):

- TF-IDF + LogReg: 10/20 (50%)
- afro-xlmr-mini fine-tuned: 7/20 (35%)
- Zero-shot gpt-oss-120b: 19/20 (95%)

† Partial run: the Groq free tier's 200k tokens/day quota ran out after generating the dataset. The scored subset is listed in the JSON (`subset_ids`); per-language/macro numbers on a one-class subset are not meaningful. Finish with `python scripts/eval_llm.py --split test` (resumable).

### Reading these numbers honestly
- The test split comes from the same generator as train, so it **over-estimates** real-world accuracy.
- The hand-written challenge set is the better proxy: the small local models miss many short, conversational scams (especially in Igbo and Hausa), which is why the app uses the local model as a fast first pass and an LLM for the final verdict and explanation.
- The single biggest improvement will come from **real, consented, anonymised messages** — see CONTRIBUTING.md.
